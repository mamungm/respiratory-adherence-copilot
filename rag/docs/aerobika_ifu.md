# Aerobika OPEP Device — Instructions for Use (sample)

## What it is

An oscillating positive expiratory pressure (OPEP) device used for airway
clearance — it helps loosen and move mucus out of the lungs by creating
gentle back-pressure and vibration during exhalation. Commonly used by
patients with COPD, bronchiectasis, or cystic fibrosis.

## Using the device

1. Sit upright in a comfortable position.
2. Take a breath that's deeper than normal, but not a full maximal breath.
3. Place the mouthpiece in the mouth and form a tight seal with the lips.
4. Exhale slowly and steadily through the device for 3-4 seconds — long
   enough to feel the vibration, not a quick huff.
5. Repeat for 10-15 breaths, then remove the device and do 2-3 huff coughs
   to help clear loosened mucus.
6. Repeat the full cycle 2-3 times per session, as prescribed.

## Resistance settings

The device has an adjustable dial that changes the resistance level.
Lower settings are easier to exhale against and are typically used when
starting out or during a flare-up; higher settings increase the oscillation
effect but require more effort. A clinician typically recommends a
starting level based on lung function.

## Common technique errors

- **Exhaling too fast (huffing) during the main breathing cycles** —
  reduces the oscillation effect; the exhale should be slow and controlled,
  not forceful.
- **Not sealing the lips around the mouthpiece** — air escapes around the
  mouth instead of through the device, and the resistance/vibration effect
  is lost.
- **Skipping the huff-cough step** — the device loosens mucus, but huff
  coughing is what actually clears it; without it, loosened secretions can
  stay in the airway.
- **Using an inappropriate resistance setting** — a setting that's too high
  can cause fatigue and early stopping; too low may not generate enough
  oscillation to be effective.

## Cleaning and maintenance

Disassemble and wash all parts in warm, soapy water after each use if
prescribed for daily airway clearance, or per the specific cleaning
schedule given by the prescribing clinician. Air dry fully before
reassembling. Replace the device per the manufacturer's recommended
replacement interval or sooner if any part is cracked or the resistance
dial no longer holds its setting.

## When to contact a clinician

Contact a healthcare provider if mucus becomes noticeably thicker, changes
color, or if breathlessness increases despite regular use of the device.
